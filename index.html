:/kafeterija/2026

        
Promo desk

        
Tells us whether a promo on Wolt or Glovo will pay for itself, then sends the emails that start it and tracks the sales.

        
Kaže nam da li se akcija na Woltu ili Glovu isplati, pa pošalje mejlove kojima se pušta i prati prodaju.

        
    
          
  • The simulator works out, for any item or combo, how many times more it has to sell to break even and how many times more it probably will. The second number is estimated from past promos.
    • 
          
  • Launching a promo is one email per step. I can preview each one, edit it and send it to myself as a test, and a sent email can't go out twice.
    • 
          
  • Once the promo is live, it puts daily sales from the register database next to what the simulator expected.
    •

Brain bottlenecks

        
I ran max-flow / min-cut on the full wiring diagram of a male fruit fly's nervous system, to find where the path from a sense to a movement is narrowest.

        
Pustio sam max-flow / min-cut kroz kompletnu mapu nervnog sistema mužjaka vinske mušice, da nađem gde je put od čula do pokreta najuži.

        
    
          
  • 174,386 neurons and about 25.8 million connections as a flow network, one behaviour at a time.
    • 
          
  • For escaping a looming shadow most of the cut lands on LPLC2 and LC4, the cells biologists already link to that escape. Nobody told the algorithm about them.
    • 
          
  • Silencing the 34 neurons in the cut between taste and the proboscis takes its motor neuron from 107.7 Hz to 0.38 Hz. 34 random neurons on the same paths change nothing.
    • 
          
  • The whole-brain simulation is rewritten in numba and fits in 16 GB of RAM on a home PC.
    • 
          
  • Vision, the 3D pictures and a proper write-up are still open.
    • 
        

        
    
          
  • 174.386 neurona i oko 25,8 miliona veza kao mreža protoka, po jedno ponašanje.
    • 
          
  • Kod bežanja od senke koja se približava najveći deo preseka pada na LPLC2 i LC4, ćelije koje biolozi već vezuju za to bežanje. Algoritmu ih niko nije pomenuo.
    • 
          
  • Kad utišam 34 neurona iz preseka između ukusa i surle, njen motorni neuron padne sa 107,7 Hz na 0,38 Hz. Nasumična 34 neurona sa istih puteva ne promene ništa.
    • 
          
  • Simulacija celog mozga je prepisana u numba i staje u 16 GB RAM-a na kućnom računaru.
    • 
          
  • Vid, 3D slike i pravi tekst o svemu su još otvoreni.
    •
