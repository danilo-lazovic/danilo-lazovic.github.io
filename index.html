Google reviews command center for an 80+ location chain

Centar za Google recenzije lanca od 80+ lokala

Reviews were spread across dozens of Google profiles. Replies were written by hand, and managers needed Google access just to answer a guest. I turned that into one inbox.

Recenzije su bile rasute po desetinama Google profila, odgovori su se pisali ručno, a menadžeri su morali da imaju Google pristup samo da bi odgovorili gostu. Od toga sam napravio jedno sanduče.

  • Full sync from 2016 onwards through three Google Business Profile APIs, with throttling, exponential backoff and per-location fault isolation, so one bad profile never stops the run.
  • AI reply drafts (Claude API) that learn each manager's own voice. Their past replies go into the prompt as examples, after filtering out templates and near-duplicates. Prompt caching keeps the cost low.
  • One-click publishing to Google on behalf of the company. Managers log in with email only, see only their own locations, and every reply is audited.
  • Stats by manager and store format, a map of the chain, and a weekly Excel report in exactly the format the team used before.
  • Started as a single Python script and is now FastAPI + Next.js, Dockerised behind Caddy with automated backups and a test suite.
  • Kompletan sync od 2016. kroz tri Google Business Profile API-ja, sa throttlingom, exponential backoffom i izolacijom grešaka po lokaciji: jedan loš profil nikad ne obori ceo prolaz.
  • AI predlozi odgovora (Claude API) koji uče stil svakog menadžera: njegovi raniji odgovori idu u prompt kao primeri (bez šablona i duplikata), a keširanje prompta drži cenu nisko.
  • Objava na Google jednim klikom u ime firme. Menadžeri se prijavljuju samo mejlom, vide samo svoje lokale, a svaki odgovor ostaje u audit logu.
  • Statistika po menadžeru i formatu lokala, mapa lanca i nedeljni Excel izveštaj tačno u formatu koji je tim ranije vodio ručno.
  • Počelo je kao jedna Python skripta, a danas je FastAPI + Next.js, u Dockeru iza Caddyja, sa automatskim backupom i testovima.

Kafeterija Portal: one screen for every channel

Kafeterija portal: jedan ekran za sve kanale

Sales lived in the POS, delivery numbers in the Wolt and Glovo dashboards, and reviews in my other tool. I'm building the internal portal that pulls all of it into one database, with roles and alarms on top.

Prodaja je živela u kasi, dostava na Wolt i Glovo portalima, a recenzije u mom drugom alatu. Pravim interni portal koji sve to sliva u jednu bazu, sa ulogama i alarmima.

  • Workers, not live calls. A POS sync pulls orders every 60 s with a 3-hour lookback for late voids and a nightly reconciliation of the full previous day. The portal only ever reads our own Postgres.
  • Automatic anomaly alarms. Each day is compared with the same weekday over the previous four weeks. A location that drops 70% raises "location stopped", and a channel that falls 15–20% raises "channel drop". The thresholds are editable by admins.
  • Honest numbers: like-for-like comparisons only, partial days compared to the same hour last week, and figures cut off at the last successful sync when data is late.
  • Four roles (admin, management, marketing, store manager), enforced in the API rather than by hiding things in the UI. An audit log records everything that leaves the portal.
  • Designed first as a full design system with mock screens for desktop and mobile, then built module by module.
  • Workeri, ne direktni pozivi. POS sync povlači porudžbine na 60 s, sa lookbackom od 3 sata za kasne storne i noćnom rekoncilijacijom celog prethodnog dana. Portal čita samo iz naše Postgres baze.
  • Automatski alarmi za anomalije. Svaki dan se poredi sa istim danom u prethodne četiri nedelje: pad lokala od 70% daje „lokal stao“, a pad kanala od 15–20% daje „pad kanala“. Pragove menja admin.
  • Poštene brojke: samo like-for-like poređenja, delimičan dan prema istom satu prošle nedelje, a kad podaci kasne, sve se seče na poslednji uspešan sync.
  • Četiri uloge (admin, uprava, marketing, menadžer lokala) koje se sprovode u API-ju, a ne sakrivanjem u UI-ju. Sve što izlazi iz portala ide u audit log.
  • Prvo je dizajniran ceo dizajn sistem sa mock ekranima za desktop i telefon, pa građen modul po modul.

Bottlenecks of the fly brain

Uska grla u mozgu mušice

The male Drosophila connectome, released in 2026, has 174,386 neurons and 25.8 million synaptic edges. I asked an operations question: which neurons are the structural bottleneck between a sense and a movement? Then I tested the answer in simulation.

Konektom mužjaka Drosophile, objavljen 2026, ima 174.386 neurona i 25,8 miliona sinaptičkih veza. Postavio sam pitanje iz operacija: koji neuroni su strukturno usko grlo između čula i pokreta? Odgovor sam onda proverio u simulaciji.

  • Hypothesis → prediction → test. Max-flow / min-cut gives the prediction, and in-silico ablation in a whole-brain spiking (LIF) model tests it.
  • Finding 1: cutting a sense off from all descending neurons just returns its first relay layer. The fly CNS is shallow, with a median of 2 hops from sense to motor. So I targeted specific behaviours instead.
  • Vision → giant fiber: the full-graph cut is dominated by LPLC2 and LC4, the neurons experiments already link to looming escape. The cut found them from wiring alone, without being told what to look for.
  • Taste → MN9 (proboscis): silencing the 34-neuron min-cut drops the motor neuron to 0.4% of its firing rate. The same number of random neurons changes nothing.
  • Re-implemented the Shiu et al. 2024 LIF model in numba with exact integration, cross-checked three max-flow solvers, and wrote unit tests for the simulator.
  • Hipoteza → predikcija → test. Max-flow / min-cut daje predikciju, a in-silico ablacija u spajking (LIF) modelu celog mozga je testira.
  • Nalaz 1: kad se čulo odseče od svih silaznih neurona, dobije se samo prvi sloj releja. Nervni sistem mušice je plitak (medijana 2 skoka od čula do motorike), pa sam ciljao konkretna ponašanja.
  • Vid → giant fiber: presek na celom grafu predvode LPLC2 i LC4, upravo neuroni koje eksperimenti već vezuju za bekstvo od pretnje. Presek ih je našao samo iz veza, bez ikakvog nagoveštaja šta da traži.
  • Ukus → MN9 (proboscis): utišavanje min-cut skupa od 34 neurona obara motorni neuron na 0,4% frekvencije. Isti broj nasumičnih neurona ne menja ništa.
  • LIF model iz Shiu et al. 2024 sam ponovo implementirao u numbi sa egzaktnom integracijom, uporedio tri max-flow solvera i napisao testove za simulator.

Colossus: directing a game studio made of AI agents

Colossus: režiram studio za igre sastavljen od AI agenata

A 3D game in the spirit of Shadow of the Colossus, built in Unity 6. I'm the Creative Director. The programmer, tech artist, 3D artist, 2D artist and playtester are Claude agents that control Unity and Blender directly over MCP.

3D igra u duhu Shadow of the Colossus, u Unity 6. Ja sam kreativni direktor, a programer, tehnički umetnik, 3D i 2D umetnik i tester su Claude agenti koji preko MCP-a direktno upravljaju Unity-jem i Blenderom.

  • Every phase answers one question, and I don't move on until the answer is yes. "Is it pleasant to run on empty terrain?" "Is grip and stamina tense?" "Is it fun to hold on to something that's trying to shake you off?"
  • Done so far: third-person movement with a Cinemachine camera, a world from CC0 assets, grip-and-stamina climbing on a 36 m tower, and climbing a moving, shaking colossus (the core test).
  • Every task is a written brief → a feature branch → a playtest report with screenshots → my review. The agents may not invent design decisions, so anything open stays marked for me.
  • Svaka faza odgovara na jedno pitanje i ne idem dalje dok odgovor nije „da“. „Da li je prijatno trčati po praznom terenu?“ „Da li su grip i stamina napeti?“ „Da li je zabavno držati se za nešto što pokušava da te strese?“
  • Urađeno: kretanje iz trećeg lica sa Cinemachine kamerom, svet od CC0 asseta, penjanje sa gripom i staminom po kuli od 36 m i penjanje po kolosu koji se kreće i trese (core test).
  • Svaki zadatak ide ovim putem: pisani brief → feature grana → izveštaj sa playtesta sa screenshotovima → moj review. Agenti ne smeju da izmišljaju dizajnerske odluke, pa sve otvoreno ostaje označeno za mene.

Principles I actually write into my projectsPrincipi koje stvarno upisujem u projekte
  1. Report every number with the base it's measured against.If a result is within noise of the random baseline, say so.
  2. Mock data and real data never mix.Every row carries its source, and switching to real data isolates the fake rows.
  3. Hypothesis → prediction → test.The graph predicts and the simulation decides, and the same goes for a sales alarm.
  4. One phase, one question.Don't move on until the answer is "yes".
  5. Don't invent what you don't know.Open decisions stay visibly open, for me and for the agents.
  1. Svaku brojku prijavi uz osnovu prema kojoj je merena.Ako je rezultat u šumu nasumične osnove, reci to.
  2. Mock i pravi podaci se nikad ne mešaju.Svaki red nosi izvor, a prelazak na prave podatke izoluje lažne.
  3. Hipoteza → predikcija → test.Graf predviđa, a simulacija presuđuje. Isto važi i za alarm prodaje.
  4. Jedna faza, jedno pitanje.Ne ide se dalje dok odgovor nije „da“.
  5. Ne izmišljaj ono što ne znaš.Otvorene odluke ostaju vidljivo otvorene, i za mene i za agente.
