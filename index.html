#01 · 2026 · graphsgrafovi

        
Brain bottlenecks

        
This one started as a question I couldn't let go of. In 2026 the full wiring diagram of a male fruit fly's nervous system came out, 174,386 neurons and about 25.8 million connections, and I wanted to see if the tools I use for production flows would say anything about a brain.

        
Ovo je počelo kao pitanje koje nisam mogao da pustim. Ove godine je objavljena kompletna mapa nervnog sistema mužjaka vinske mušice, 174.386 neurona i oko 25,8 miliona veza, i hteo sam da vidim da li alati koje koristim za protok u proizvodnji mogu nešto da kažu o mozgu.

        
I treated the connectome as a flow network and ran max-flow / min-cut from the sensory neurons to the neurons that drive movement. My first try was useless. If you cut a sense off from every motor output at once, the cut is simply the first layer of neurons after that sense, because the fly's nervous system is very shallow, about two steps from sense to muscle. So I went one behaviour at a time. For escaping a looming shadow, most of the cut landed on LPLC2 and LC4, which are the cells biologists already link to that escape. I never told the algorithm anything about them.

        
Konektom sam posmatrao kao mrežu protoka i tražio max-flow / min-cut od čulnih neurona do neurona koji pokreću telo. Prvi pokušaj nije vredeo ništa. Kad čulo odsečeš od svih motornih izlaza odjednom, presek je samo prvi sloj neurona posle tog čula, jer je nervni sistem mušice jako plitak, oko dva koraka od čula do mišića. Zato sam išao ponašanje po ponašanje. Za bekstvo od senke koja se približava, najveći deo preseka je pao na LPLC2 i LC4, upravo ćelije koje biolozi već vezuju za to bekstvo. Algoritmu nisam rekao ništa o njima.

        
Next I wanted to know if the cut actually matters, which meant simulating the whole brain. The published model runs on Brian2, and Brian2 needs a C compiler on Windows, so I rewrote the model in numba instead. All of it had to fit in the 16 GB of RAM on my home PC. When I silenced the 34 neurons in the cut between taste and the proboscis, its motor neuron went from 107.7 Hz to 0.38 Hz. Silencing 34 random neurons on the same paths did nothing, and the best centrality measure only took it down to about half.

        
Onda sam hteo da vidim da li presek stvarno nešto znači, a za to je trebala simulacija celog mozga. Objavljeni model radi na Brian2, a Brian2 na Windowsu traži C kompajler, pa sam model prepisao u numbi. Sve je moralo da stane u 16 GB RAM-a na kućnom računaru. Kad sam utišao 34 neurona iz preseka između ukusa i surle, njen motorni neuron je pao sa 107,7 Hz na 0,38 Hz. Utišavanje 34 nasumična neurona na istim putevima nije uradilo ništa, a najbolja mera centralnosti ga je spustila tek na pola.

        
Vision is still open. Every photoreceptor in the fly is histaminergic, and a model that only knows "excite" and "inhibit" can't treat them fairly. That, the 3D pictures and a proper write-up are next.

        
Vid je i dalje otvoren. Svi fotoreceptori kod mušice su histaminergični, a model koji zna samo za „pobudi“ i „koči“ ne može pošteno da ih predstavi. To, 3D slike i pravi tekst o svemu su sledeći.

#02 · 2026 · Kafeterija

        
Review engine

        
Kafeterija has 70 cafés, each with its own Google profile. When I started, replies were typed by hand, and a manager needed access to Google just to thank a guest.

        
Kafeterija ima 70 kafića, svaki sa svojim Google profilom. Kad sam počeo, odgovori su se kucali ručno, a menadžeru je trebao pristup Googleu samo da bi zahvalio gostu.

Pravimo je nas dvojica: ja vodim kod i priču uz AI agente, a drugi autor radi grafiku. Svakog dana na sto stigne nekoliko kartona. Za svaki upišeš iznos u markama i udariš jedan od tri pečata: tolerisati, pritisnuti, ukloniti. Koliko čovek stvarno zarađuje, ne znaš. Imaš jutarnju depešu, ljude na terenu, novine i kovertu samog kriminalca, a oni se ne slažu. Igra je u hvatanju tih neslaganja.

        
I've never built anything in Unity myself, I've only directed agents in it, so I wanted rules I could run and check without opening the editor. They live in plain C# that doesn't know Unity exists: money is whole numbers, time is minutes since midnight, and the core never reads the real clock. A whole working day can be played as text in a terminal, by me or by an agent, and one command plays it with a handful of simple strategies and prints what each of them ends the day with.

        
U samom Unityju nikad ništa nisam napravio, samo sam vodio agente u njemu, pa sam hteo pravila koja mogu da pokrenem i proverim bez otvaranja Editora. Zato žive u čistom C#-u koji ne zna da Unity postoji: novac su celi brojevi, vreme su minuti od ponoći, a jezgro nikad ne čita pravi sat. Ceo radni dan može da se odigra kao tekst u terminalu, bilo da igram ja ili agent, a jedna komanda ga odigra sa nekoliko prostih strategija i ispiše sa čim koja završi dan.

        
That table is above. On the test day, the only strategy that pays for the heating, the medicine and the food is the one that works out the truth and then steals. The game never forces you to steal. In hyperinflation it just keeps raising the price of staying honest.

        
Ta tabela je gore. Na probnom danu jedina strategija koja plati grejanje, lekove i hranu jeste ona koja izvede istinu pa krade. Igra nikad ne tera na krađu. U hiperinflaciji samo stalno diže cenu poštenja.

        
We also keep a list of ways the game could fail even if it works: if it glorified the criminals or the service, if you could recognise real people in the characters, if it had a triumphant ending.

Job radar

        
Architecture internships and junior jobs in Belgrade get posted in about sixty different places: job boards, the faculty's news, the professional associations, city and ministry pages, and the careers pages of around thirty studios.

        
Prakse i junior poslovi za arhitekte u Beogradu izlaze na šezdesetak različitih mesta: sajtovi za posao, vesti fakulteta, strukovna udruženja, strane grada i ministarstva i strane „Karijera“ tridesetak studija.

        
Nobody checks sixty pages every morning, so a script does. It reads all of them (the run above took twenty seconds), keeps only architecture and only Belgrade, and sorts by level: internships and junior jobs first, then ads that don't state a level, with senior ones folded away at the bottom.

        
Niko ne proverava šezdeset strana svako jutro, pa to radi skripta. Pročita ih sve (pokretanje odozgo trajalo je dvadeset sekundi), zadrži samo arhitekturu i samo Beograd, i poređa po nivou: prvo prakse i junior poslovi, zatim oglasi bez navedenog nivoa, a seniorski skupljeni na dnu.

        
Most of the work went into what not to show. The same ad on two boards becomes one card with both links. Anything past its deadline disappears, and an ad with no deadline goes when its source stops listing it, or after 45 days. If a studio's careers page has no feed, the script just watches the page and says when the text changes.

        
Najviše posla otišlo je na ono što ne treba prikazati. Isti oglas na dva sajta postaje jedna kartica sa oba linka. Sve čemu je prošao rok nestaje, a oglas bez roka nestaje kad ga izvor više ne prikazuje ili posle 45 dana. Ako strana „Karijera“ nekog studija nema feed, skripta samo prati stranu i javi kad se tekst promeni.

        
LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram can't be read this way, so the report ends with ready-made links to check by hand, and with a table of which sources worked and which one broke because a site changed its layout.

        
LinkedIn, Facebook i Instagram ne mogu da se čitaju ovako, pa se izveštaj završava gotovim linkovima za ručnu proveru i tabelom koja pokazuje koji je izvor radio, a koji je pukao jer je sajt promenio izgled.
