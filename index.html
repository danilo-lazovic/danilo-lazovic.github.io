#01 · 2026 · graphsgrafovi

        
Brain bottlenecks

        
In 2026 the full wiring of a male fruit fly's nervous system was published: 174,386 neurons and 25.8 million connections. I wanted to know where the path from a sense to a movement gets squeezed. It's the same question I ask about a production line.

        
Ove godine je objavljena kompletna mapa nervnog sistema mužjaka vinske mušice: 174.386 neurona i 25,8 miliona veza. Hteo sam da znam gde se put od čula do pokreta sužava. Isto pitanje postavljam i za proizvodnu liniju.

        
I treated the connectome as a flow network and ran max-flow and min-cut between sensory neurons and the neurons that drive movement. Cutting a sense off from every motor output told me nothing. The fly is shallow, about two hops from sense to muscle, so the cut was just the first layer. Aiming at one behaviour at a time worked. For the escape from a looming shadow, the cut was led by LPLC2 and LC4, the same cells biologists already tie to that escape. The graph found them from the wiring alone.

        
Konektom sam posmatrao kao mrežu protoka i tražio max-flow i min-cut između čulnih neurona i neurona koji pokreću telo. Kad sam čulo odsekao od svih motornih izlaza, nisam dobio ništa korisno. Mušica je plitka, oko dva koraka od čula do mišića, pa je presek bio samo prvi sloj. Kad sam ciljao jedno ponašanje, proradilo je. Za bekstvo od senke koja se približava, presek su predvodili LPLC2 i LC4, upravo ćelije koje biolozi već vezuju za to bekstvo. Graf ih je našao samo iz veza.

        
Then I tested the prediction. I rewrote a whole-brain spiking model in numba and silenced the 34 neurons in the cut between taste and the proboscis. Its motor neuron dropped from 107.7 Hz to 0.38 Hz. Silencing 34 random neurons on the same paths changed nothing, and the best centrality measure only cut it by about half.

        
Onda sam predikciju proverio. Prepisao sam spajking model celog mozga u numbi i utišao 34 neurona iz preseka između ukusa i surle. Njen motorni neuron je pao sa 107,7 Hz na 0,38 Hz. Kad sam utišao 34 nasumična neurona na istim putevima, ništa se nije promenilo, a najbolja mera centralnosti ga je oborila tek na pola.

      

      

        

          
stack

          
Python, OR-Tools, igraph, SciPy, numba, neuPrint

          
datapodaci

          
Male CNS v1.0 connectome, Janelia FlyEM and Google

          
Konektom male CNS v1.0, Janelia FlyEM i Google

#02 · 2026 · Kafeterija

        
Review engine

        
Kafeterija has 70 cafés, and each one has its own Google profile. Reviews piled up across all of them, replies were typed by hand, and a manager needed Google access just to thank a guest.

        
Kafeterija ima 70 kafića i svaki ima svoj Google profil. Recenzije su se gomilale po svima njima, odgovori su se kucali ručno, a menadžer je morao da ima Google pristup samo da bi zahvalio gostu.

        
It started as one Python script that pulled reviews into a spreadsheet. Today it's a FastAPI and Next.js app. It syncs all 47,000 reviews going back to 2016, puts the new ones in a single inbox and publishes replies straight to Google in the company's name. Managers sign in with their email and only see their own cafés.

        
Počelo je kao jedna Python skripta koja je vukla recenzije u tabelu. Danas je to FastAPI i Next.js aplikacija. Sinhronizuje svih 47.000 recenzija unazad do 2016, nove stavlja u jedno sanduče i odgovore objavljuje direktno na Google u ime firme. Menadžeri se prijavljuju mejlom i vide samo svoje kafiće.

        
My favourite part is the reply drafts. Claude writes a suggestion, and with it I send a few of that manager's own earlier replies, so the draft sounds like them. They read it, fix what they want and send it. Once a week the app also builds the Excel report the team used to fill in by hand, in exactly the same format, so nobody had to change how they work.

        
Najdraži deo su mi predlozi odgovora. Claude piše predlog, a uz njega šaljem nekoliko ranijih odgovora tog menadžera, pa predlog zvuči kao on. Menadžer pročita, ispravi šta hoće i pošalje. Jednom nedeljno aplikacija pravi i Excel izveštaj koji je tim ranije popunjavao ručno, u potpuno istom formatu, pa niko nije morao da menja način rada.

Café portal

So I'm building an internal portal that pulls everything into one Postgres database. Small workers do the pulling: register orders every minute, platform exports every morning, reviews from the review engine. The portal only ever reads our own database, so when Wolt is down, sales from the registers still load.

        
Zato pravim interni portal koji sve sliva u jednu Postgres bazu. Mali workeri povlače podatke: porudžbine sa kasa svakog minuta, izvoze sa platformi svako jutro, recenzije iz mog drugog alata. Portal čita samo našu bazu, pa kad Wolt ne radi, prodaja sa kasa se i dalje učitava.

        
Every day is compared with the same weekday over the last four weeks. If a café drops by 70%, the portal says it stopped. If a whole channel drops, it says that too. I drew every screen first, with fake numbers clearly marked as fake, and now I build it one module at a time.

        
Svaki dan se poredi sa istim danom u prethodne četiri nedelje. Ako kafić padne za 70%, portal kaže da je stao. Ako padne ceo kanal, kaže i to. Prvo sam nacrtao svaki ekran, sa izmišljenim brojkama jasno označenim, a sada ga gradim modul po modul.

Ispod stoji model za izveštavanje u Power BI-ju i Excelu. Ima tri sloja (dimenzije, sirovi podaci, izveštaj), pa je novi mesec samo ubacivanje podataka, a ne pravljenje ispočetka. Prati ulaganje u oglase po kanalu i deli meni u BCG matricu: šta se prodaje i zarađuje, a šta samo zauzima mesto.

Demand goes up and down every week, capacity doesn't. A plan has to decide what gets made now and what can wait.

        
Tražnja svake nedelje ide gore i dole, kapacitet ne. Plan mora da odluči šta se pravi sada, a šta može da sačeka.

        
At university I built a planner in MATLAB. It reads weekly demand and daily capacity from Excel, releases production so the backlog is cleared first, avoids making stock too early, and draws nine charts that show what happened. The drawing is one of them: stock above the line, backlog below it.

        
Na fakultetu sam napravio planer u MATLAB-u. Čita nedeljnu tražnju i dnevni kapacitet iz Excela, pušta proizvodnju tako da se prvo skine backlog, ne pravi zalihe prerano i crta devet grafika koji pokazuju šta se desilo. Crtež je jedan od njih: zalihe iznad linije, backlog ispod.

        
At Bosch I did it for real. I planned and ordered production materials in SAP, and next to that I built a desktop app in Python and PyQt5 that plans more than 800 SKUs over 52 weeks, a VBA tool that splits capacity in Excel, and Power Query automation that feeds Power BI.

        
U Boschu sam to radio zaista. Planirao sam i naručivao materijal u SAP-u, a pored toga napravio desktop aplikaciju u Pythonu i PyQt5 koja planira više od 800 artikala na 52 nedelje, VBA alat koji deli kapacitet u Excelu i Power Query automatizaciju za Power BI.

#07 · 2025 · heuristicsheuristike

        
Travelling salesman

        
Seventeen cities, from Kosjerić to Minsk. Which order is the shortest?

        
Sedamnaest gradova, od Kosjerića do Minska. Koji redosled je najkraći?

        
I geocoded the cities in Python and wrote four heuristics in MATLAB: nearest neighbour, 2-opt, simulated annealing with 50,000 iterations, and a genetic algorithm with order crossover. I expected the genetic algorithm to win. It didn't. Plain 2-opt found the shortest tour, 68.46 against 74.92 for the genetic algorithm and 79.75 for nearest neighbour. On a problem this size, a simple local search was enough.

        
Gradove sam geokodirao u Pythonu i napisao četiri heuristike u MATLAB-u: najbližeg suseda, 2-opt, simulirano kaljenje sa 50.000 iteracija i genetski algoritam sa order crossoverom. Očekivao sam da pobedi genetski. Nije. Obični 2-opt je našao najkraću turu, 68,46 prema 74,92 za genetski i 79,75 za najbližeg suseda. Na ovoliko malom problemu, jednostavna lokalna pretraga je bila dovoljna.
